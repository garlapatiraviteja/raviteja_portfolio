About me

I build AI that thinks in groups, watches closely, and learns from almost nothing. My systems borrow from swarms, hives and ghosts: agents that decide together, networks that route like colonies, models that spot what is hiding in a crowd.

My projects cover the full loop of applied AI. Seeing: FaceFit 3D, Cipher Yūrei and real-time sign language recognition. Defending: Operation Mirror Match, an adversarial fraud detection system. Deciding: Swarm Brain, Network Hive and an AI trading system that blends technical indicators, local LLMs, multi-agent analysis and evolutionary methods.

Then there is Graph Ghost, my experiment in learning with zero training through graph-based semi-supervised learning. I like the problems where the textbook answer is not enough.
